# Verbs: the standard verbs of naturalloy

naturalloy gives the forms of these verbs. A reading of any canon can mark one with no verb term of its own: `{1} ^holds^ {2}`. Each is a term: its statement says what the verb means, and its grammar table gives its third person and its participle. Its stem is its infinitive. `node test/core/verbs.ts --write` writes them into `src/core/verbs.ts`, and a test fails where the two differ.

A canon that uses a verb in a sense of its own defines the verb as a term. A mark that links to that term takes its forms instead.
